## Principles for teaching and implementation

- A consonant normally carries an inherent vowel; a bare written consonant is not merely an English-style consonant sound.
- Distinguish a letter's full name from its reading sound. The user wants reading sounds in the listening games.
- Vowel signs, tone marks, and medials have different functions. They are not interchangeable decorations.
- Do not teach tone contrasts merely as short versus long vowels. Pitch and voice quality are also relevant.
- English or Chinese pronunciation hints cannot replace verified Myanmar audio.
- Use complete syllables as recorded units. Do not concatenate a consonant recording and a vowel recording and assume the result has correct pronunciation.
- Select spelling appropriate to the consonant: ကာ and ခါ demonstrate different shapes of the AA sign. Do not append ာ universally.
- Letters that share a reading sound are still distinct spellings. Avoid indistinguishable distractors in an audio-only quiz; teach spelling separately with meaning/context.
- Follow the textbook's actual sequence. The user's requested app lesson names need not match the textbook's chapter numbering.
